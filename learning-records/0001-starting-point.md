# Starting point: receptive knowledge, weak live production

**Prior knowledge:** He studied tenses and modal verbs years ago and can read simple English with effort. He understands some work English and owns three grammar books (Murphy Intermediate, Wren & Martin, Word Power Made Easy), but has finished none.

**Baseline:** In live client calls and casual conversation he cannot form sentences in real time and sometimes cannot speak at all. Pronunciation is unclear to him. He reads English slowly and does not always understand what he reads.

**Why it matters:** Future sessions should not re-teach grammar rules as knowledge. The gap is automatic production of high-frequency patterns under mild time pressure. Start with concrete introductions and work scenarios, short sentences, and heavy repetition. Avoid long explanations and isolated vocabulary lists.
