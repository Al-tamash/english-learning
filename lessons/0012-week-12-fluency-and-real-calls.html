The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. Let me summarize.

Call ke end me points short me repeat karo.

+
Let me summarize the main points.
−
I don't have a full summary yet.
?
Can you summarize the call?
  • Let me summarize what we decided.Main summarize karta hoon kya decide hua.
  • Let me summarize the three action items.Main teen action items summarize karta hoon.

Grammar: Let me + base verb. summarize = short me repeat karna. Summary me 2–3 points, not story.

Common mistake: “Let me to summarize” → “Let me summarize.” let me ke baad base verb.

2. The main point is ___.

Ek sabse important point nikalo.

+
The main point is the deadline.
−
The main point isn't the price.
?
What is the main point?
  • The main point is the launch date.Main point launch date hai.
  • The main point is the client's approval.Main point client ki approval hai.

Grammar: The main point is + noun/clause. Ek point, five nahi.

Common mistake: “The main point is about the deadline” → “The main point is the deadline.” Direct noun better.

3. Before we finish, ___.

Call close karne se pehle last item.

+
Before we finish, let's confirm the next call.
−
Before we finish, let's not forget the payment question.
?
Before we finish, can we confirm the date?
  • Before we finish, I have one question.Finish karne se pehle, ek sawaal hai.
  • Before we finish, let's agree on the next step.Finish se pehle next step par agree karein.

Grammar: Before + subject + verb, + main clause. before we finish = call end hone se pehle.

Common mistake: “Before finish, let's confirm” → “Before we finish, let's confirm.” Subject chahiye.

4. I'll follow up with ___.

Call ke baad kaam ka owner bolo.

+
I'll follow up with the client tomorrow.
−
I won't follow up until Monday.
?
Who will follow up?
  • I'll follow up with the design team.Main design team se follow up karunga.
  • I'll follow up on the invoice.Main invoice par follow up karunga.

Grammar: follow up with + person; follow up on + topic. Future promise.

Common mistake: “I'll follow up to the client” → “I'll follow up with the client.” with person.

5. Could we schedule ___?

Next call ka time set karo.

+
Could we schedule the next call for Monday?
−
We can't schedule it this week.
?
Could we schedule a call?
  • Could we schedule a demo for Thursday?Kya hum Thursday ke liye demo schedule kar sakte hain?
  • Could we schedule the review for next week?Kya hum review next week ke liye rakh sakte hain?

Grammar: Could we + base verb. schedule something for + time.

Common mistake: “Could we schedule to Monday?” → “Could we schedule it for Monday?” for + time.

6. Thanks for your time.

Call close karne ki simple, professional line.

+
Thanks for your time.
−
I don't want to take more of your time.
?
Do you have five more minutes?
  • Thanks for your time. I'll send the notes.Aapke time ke liye thanks. Main notes bhej doonga.
  • Thanks for your time today.Aaj ke time ke liye thanks.

Grammar: Thanks for + noun/-ing. Call close karne ka polite line. time uncountable.

Common mistake: “Thanks for your times” → “Thanks for your time.” Time uncountable.

Practice 1: pattern fill-ins

Blank me ek word likho. Capital letters aur extra spaces ki chinta mat karo. Check dabao, feedback dekho, phir agla.

Practice 2: grammar fill-ins

Ek word ya short form likho. Contractions aur full forms dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Practice 4: Hinglish → English

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

Model dialogue

Final mock client call. Riya = client. Summary, next step, schedule aur close — poora structure. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Okay, I think we have covered everything.

You

Let me summarize. The main point is the launch date — the 15th.

Riya

Yes, and we need the content by the 10th.

You

Correct. Before we finish, let's confirm the next call.

Riya

How about Monday at four?

You

That works. Could we schedule it for Monday at four?

Riya

Sure. Can you send the notes?

You

Yes, I'll follow up with the notes today. Thanks for your time.

Poora call: open → discuss → summarize → next step → schedule → thanks. Ye structure roz use karo.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Summarize

Call ke teen points summarize karo.

Let me summarize. The main point is ___ . We also agreed on ___ .

2. Confirm action items

Kaun kya karega, bolo.

I'll follow up with ___ . You will send ___ by ___ .

3. Schedule next call

Next meeting set karo.

Could we schedule the next call for ___ ? Does that work for you?

4. Full 2-minute call

Poora mock call record karo.

Hi, thanks for joining. Let's start with ___ . ... Let me summarize. ... Thanks for your time.

Vocabulary in action

Sirf word yaad nahi — collocation ke saath bolo. Apne 60-second answer me do words use karo.

summary

a quick summary

Let me give you a quick summary.

action item

the action items

We have three action items.

follow-up

a follow-up email

I'll send a follow-up email.

schedule

schedule a call

Let's schedule a call for Monday.

decide

decide the next step

Let's decide the next step.

next step

the next step

The next step is the design.

60-second self-check

Phone me 60 seconds record karo. Neeche checklist se score karo; 5/6 = ready.

3/6 = ek pattern dobara bolo, phir record karo. Recording suno, ek cheez sudharo, dobara record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

Pausing

Let me summarize — the main point is — the deadline. Pause se listener ko process karne ka time milta hai.

Rhythm

Content words par stress: LET me SUMmarize the MAIN POINT. Function words halke.

Final intonation

Statements me voice neeche. “Thanks for your time.” — end me confident fall.

Confidence

Speed slow rakho. Bhool gaye to “Let me check that and follow up” bolo — pause se better hai.
