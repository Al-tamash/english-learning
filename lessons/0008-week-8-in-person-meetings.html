The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. Nice to meet you.

Pehli mulaqat par fixed phrase. End me: Nice meeting you.

+
Nice to meet you, Riya.
−
It's not our first meeting — we spoke on Zoom.
?
Is this your first visit to our office?
  • Nice to meet you. Thank you for coming.Aapse mil kar achha laga. Aane ke liye thanks.
  • It's nice to meet you too.Aap se milkar bhi achha laga.

Grammar: Nice to meet you = pehli mulaqat. Meeting ke end me Nice meeting you. Response: Nice to meet you too.

Common mistake: “Nice to meet to you” → “Nice to meet you.” meet ke baad to nahi.

2. Let me show you ___.

Office, demo ya product dikhane ka offer.

+
Let me show you our office.
−
I can't show you the server room.
?
Can you show me the design?
  • Let me show you the demo.Main aapko demo dikhata hoon.
  • Let me show you our new office.Main aapko hamara naya office dikhata hoon.

Grammar: Let me + base verb + object. show someone something.

Common mistake: “Let me to show you” → “Let me show you.” let me ke baad base verb.

3. Would you like ___?

Polite offer — chai, paani, seat ya demo.

+
Would you like some tea?
−
No, thank you. I don't want anything right now.
?
Would you like to see the demo?
  • Would you like some water?Kya aap paani lena chahenge?
  • Would you like to sit down?Kya aap baithna chahenge?

Grammar: Would you like + noun / to + verb. Polite offer. Answer: Yes, please. / No, thank you.

Common mistake: “Do you like some water?” (offer) → “Would you like some water?” Offer ke liye would.

4. We can offer ___.

Price, discount ya package batane ke liye.

+
We can offer a ten percent discount.
−
We can't offer free hosting.
?
What can you offer?
  • We can offer a monthly plan.Hum monthly plan de sakte hain.
  • We can offer two designs.Hum do designs de sakte hain.

Grammar: can offer + noun. Negotiation me range batao, promise zyada mat karo.

Common mistake: “We can offer to you a discount” → “We can offer you a discount.” offer + person + thing.

5. Here is ___.

Kuch dete time — card, list, sample.

+
Here is our price list.
−
This isn't the final quote.
?
Is this the final price?
  • Here is my card.Ye mera card hai.
  • Here are the samples.Ye samples hain.

Grammar: Here is + singular; Here are + plural. Handing something over.

Common mistake: “Here is our samples” → “Here are our samples.” Plural ke liye here are.

6. What do you think?

Client ki opinion poochho — meeting ko interactive banao.

+
What do you think about the design?
−
I'm not sure what you think.
?
What do you think?
  • What do you think of the new homepage?Naye homepage ke baare me kya sochte hain?
  • What do you think? Should we change the colour?Aap kya sochte hain? Colour badlein?

Grammar: What do you think of/about + noun? Think ke baad opinion. Alone bhi chalega.

Common mistake: “What do you think about?” → “What do you think?” ya “What do you think about it?”

Ek word ya short form likho. Contractions aur full forms dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

Nice to meet you. Thank you for coming.

Let me show you our office.

Would you like some tea?

We can offer you a ten percent discount.

Model dialogue

Client office visit. Riya = client. Welcome, tour, demo, offer aur goodbye — sab cover karo. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Hello! We have a meeting at eleven.

You

Welcome. Nice to meet you. Let me show you our office first.

Riya

Thank you. The office looks nice.

You

Would you like some tea before the demo?

Riya

Yes, please. And what is the price?

You

Here is our price list. We can offer you a ten percent discount for the first year.

Riya

That sounds good. What do you think about the timeline?

You

I think two weeks is realistic. I'll send you the final quote by Monday.

In-person meeting me chai, demo, price aur next step — chaar moments. Har moment ke liye ek line yaad rakho.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

2. Offer something

Pronunciation quick check

Polite intonation

Would you LIKE some TEA? — voice end me soft up. Demand nahi, offer lagta hai.

Prices

ten percent = /ten pəˈsent/. Fifteen vs fifty: fifTEEN (stress end), FIFty (stress start).

Linking

meet you → /miːtʃuː/; would you → /wʊdʒə/. In-person me bhi natural linking chahiye.

Numbers

1,500 = fifteen hundred; 15,000 = fifteen thousand. Lakh/crore ko client ke saath clear karo.
