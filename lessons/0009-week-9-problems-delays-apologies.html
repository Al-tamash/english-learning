The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. I'm sorry for the delay.

Delay ke liye seedhi, simple apology.

+
I'm sorry for the delay.
−
I'm not happy about the delay either.
?
Is there a delay?
  • I'm sorry for the late reply.Late reply ke liye sorry.
  • Sorry for the confusion.Confusion ke liye sorry.

Grammar: sorry for + noun/-ing. sorry to + verb: I'm sorry to say this. Delay ke liye for.

Common mistake: “Sorry for delay” → “Sorry for the delay.” Article the chahiye.

2. There was an issue with ___.

Problem kahan thi, clearly bolo.

+
There was an issue with the payment gateway.
−
There wasn't any issue with the design.
?
Was there an issue with the server?
  • There was an issue with the backup.Backup me problem thi.
  • There were two issues with the form.Form me do problems thi.

Grammar: There was + singular; there were + plural. issue with + thing.

Common mistake: “There was issue with server” → “There was an issue with the server.” Article a/the.

3. It's delayed because ___.

Reason do — blame nahi, fact.

+
It's delayed because the content is not ready.
−
It's not delayed because of us.
?
Why is it delayed?
  • It's delayed because the client changed the design.Delay hui kyunki client ne design badla.
  • It's delayed because of the holiday.Holiday ki wajah se delay hui.

Grammar: because + clause (subject + verb). because of + noun. Difference clear rakho.

Common mistake: “because the content not ready” → “because the content is not ready.” Clause me verb chahiye.

4. We're fixing it.

Team abhi action me hai — ye confidence deta hai.

+
We're fixing it right now.
−
We're not fixing the design today.
?
When will it be fixed?
  • We're fixing the login bug now.Hum login bug abhi theek kar rahe hain.
  • It will be fixed by evening.Ye shaam tak theek ho jayega.

Grammar: Present continuous for current action. fix = theek karna. Passive: it will be fixed.

Common mistake: “We fixing it” → “We're fixing it.” be verb chahiye.

5. I'll update you by ___.

Client ko next information ka time do.

+
I'll update you by 4 p.m. today.
−
I won't have an update before lunch.
?
When will you update me?
  • I'll update you by Friday afternoon.Main Friday dopahar tak update doonga.
  • I'll update you as soon as I know.Jaise hi pata chalega, update doonga.

Grammar: update someone by + time. by = deadline. update + person, no to.

Common mistake: “I'll update to you” → “I'll update you.” update + person.

6. Thank you for your patience.

Client ke sabr ko recognize karo — call close karne ke liye.

+
Thank you for your patience.
−
Sorry for the confusion.
?
Can you give us a little more time?
  • Thank you for waiting.Wait karne ke liye thanks.
  • Thank you for your understanding.Samajhne ke liye thanks.

Grammar: Thank you for + noun/-ing. patience = sabr, /ˈpeɪʃns/.

Common mistake: “Thank you for your patient” → “Thank you for your patience.” Patient = mareez; patience = sabr.

Grammar corner

Ye rules Week 9 ke saare patterns cover karte hain. Ratta nahi — bolte time apply karo.

RuleExample
sorry for + noun/-ingI'm sorry for the delay.
There was/were + noun; issue withThere was an issue with the server.
because + clause; because of + nounIt's delayed because the content is late.
Present continuous for a current fixWe're fixing it right now.
update + person (no to)I'll update you by Friday.
Thank you for + noun/-ingThank you for your patience.

Common mistakes

Ye 6 galtiyan Week 9 me sabse zyada hoti hain. Pehle wrong bolo, phir right — loud.

✗ Sorry for delay.

✓ Sorry for the delay.

Article the.

✗ There was issue with server.

✓ There was an issue with the server.

a/the article.

✗ It's delayed because the content not ready.

✓ It's delayed because the content is not ready.

Clause me verb.

✗ We fixing it.

✓ We're fixing it.

be + verb-ing.

✗ I'll update to you by Friday.

✓ I'll update you by Friday.

update + person, to nahi.

✗ Thank you for your patient.

✓ Thank you for your patience.

patience = sabr.

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

I'm sorry for the delay.

There was an issue with the server.

We're fixing it right now.

I'll update you by 4 p.m. today.

Model dialogue

Client ka site down hai. Riya = client. Sorry, reason, fix time aur next update — chaar steps. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

The site is still down. What is happening?

You

I'm sorry for the delay. There was an issue with the server.

Riya

This is the second time this month.

You

I understand. We're fixing it right now. It's delayed because the backup took longer than expected.

Riya

When will it be fixed?

You

It will be fixed in about one hour. I'll update you by 4 p.m.

Riya

Okay. Please keep me posted.

You

Thank you for your patience. I'll message you as soon as it is live.

Apology ka structure: sorry + reason + fix + next update. Blame mat do, plan do.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Apologize

Delay ke liye sorry bolo.

I'm sorry for the delay. There was an issue with ___ .

2. Explain the reason

Reason clear bolo, blame ke bina.

It's delayed because ___ . We're fixing it now.

3. Give a fix time

Recovery plan batao.

It will be fixed in ___ . I'll update you by ___ .

4. Calm an angry client

Chaar line ka apology record karo.

I understand. I'm sorry for ___ . We're fixing ___ . I'll update you by ___ . Thank you for your patience.

Vocabulary in action

Sirf word yaad nahi — collocation ke saath bolo. Apne 60-second answer me do words use karo.

issue

a technical issue

There was a technical issue with the server.

bug

fix a bug

We fixed the login bug.

delay

a short delay

There is a short delay in testing.

fix

fix the problem

We're fixing the problem now.

solve

solve the issue

We solved the issue in one hour.

mistake

a small mistake

There was a small mistake in the invoice.

Pronunciation quick check

Tone in apologies

I'm SO sorry for the deLAY. Voice thodi neeche, slow. Fast apology rude lagti hai.

patience

/ˈpeɪʃns/ — PEI-shns. ‘patient’ (/ˈpeɪʃnt/) se ending different hai.

because

/bɪˈkɒz/ — stress second syllable. “be-CUZ” nahi, “be-CAWZ”.

Pausing

Sorry — there was an issue — we're fixing it. Pause se message clear aur calm lagta hai.
