The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. I usually ___ at ___.

Usually + verb; time ke pehle at.

+
I usually check my email at nine.
−
I don't usually check email at night.
?
What do you usually do in the morning?
  • I usually start work at ten.Main aam taur par das baje kaam shuru karta hoon.
  • She usually takes the metro to the office.Woh aam taur par office metro se jaati hai.

Grammar: Present simple + frequency adverb. usually/always/often/sometimes/never verb se pehle aate hain: I usually work. am/is/are ke baad: I'm usually busy.

Common mistake: “I am usually go to office at 10” → “I usually go to office at 10.” Usually ke saath base verb.

2. I don't ___ very often.

Negative habit; very often = bahut zyada baar nahi.

+
I watch cricket almost every weekend.
−
I don't watch cricket very often.
?
Do you watch cricket very often?
  • I don't go to the gym very often.Main bahut zyada baar gym nahi jaata.
  • We don't eat out very often.Hum bahut zyada baar bahar nahi khaate.

Grammar: don't/doesn't + base verb. very often sentence ke end me. Not very often = rarely.

Common mistake: “I don't watched cricket” → “I don't watch cricket.” don't ke baad base verb.

3. How was your weekend?

Weekend khatam ho gaya, isliye was. Aaj ke liye How is your day?

+
It was good, thanks.
−
It wasn't very relaxing.
?
How was your weekend?
  • It was busy, but I enjoyed it.Vyast tha, lekin maza aaya.
  • My weekend was quiet. I stayed home.Mera weekend shaant tha. Main ghar par ruka.

Grammar: Past simple be: was/were. Weekend singular → was; days/events plural → were.

Common mistake: “How is your weekend?” (Monday ko) → “How was your weekend?” Weekend past me khatam ho gaya.

4. I went to ___ / I stayed ___.

went = go ka past; stayed = ghar ya kisi jagah ruka.

+
I went to my cousin's place and stayed there for two hours.
−
I didn't go out on Sunday.
?
Where did you go on Sunday?
  • I went to the market in the morning.Main subah market gaya.
  • We stayed home and watched a movie.Hum ghar par ruke aur movie dekhi.

Grammar: Past simple irregular: go → went. stay → stayed. ghar ke liye: I stayed home / I stayed at home.

Common mistake: “I went to home” → “I went home.” Home ke pehle to nahi lagta.

5. Do you like ___?

Like ke baad noun ya verb+ing.

+
I like working from home.
−
I don't like waking up early.
?
Do you like working from home?
  • Do you like tea or coffee?Aapko chai pasand hai ya coffee?
  • I like watching movies on Sunday.Mujhe Sunday ko movie dekhna pasand hai.

Grammar: like + noun / verb-ing. Short answer: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Common mistake: “Do you like work from home?” → “Do you like working from home?” Like ke baad verb-ing.

6. What about you?

Same question wapas poochhne ka short tarika.

+
I'm a developer. What about you?
−
I'm not very busy today. What about you?
?
What about you?
  • I usually commute by bike. What about you?Main aam taur par bike se jaata hoon. Tum?
  • I like cricket. What about you?Mujhe cricket pasand hai. Tumhe?

Grammar: What about you? = And you? Same topic continue karta hai. Formal me And you? bhi chalega.

Common mistake: “What about your?” → “What about you?” You subject aur object dono me same rehta hai.

Grammar corner

Ye rules Week 2 ke saare patterns cover karte hain. Ratta nahi — bolte time apply karo.

RuleExample
usually/always/often/sometimes/never + base verbI usually start at ten.
am/is/are + usually + adjective/nounI'm usually busy on Monday.
don't/doesn't + base verbI don't watch TV very often.
Past simple: was/were; went/stayedIt was good. I went home.
like + noun / verb-ingI like working from home.
Questions: Do you + base verb? / How was ___?Do you like tea? How was your weekend?

Common mistakes

Ye 6 galtiyan Week 2 me sabse zyada hoti hain. Pehle wrong bolo, phir right — loud.

✗ I am usually go to office at 10.

✓ I usually go to office at 10.

Frequency adverb ke baad base verb.

✗ I don't watched cricket.

✓ I don't watch cricket.

don't ke baad base verb.

✗ How is your weekend?

✓ How was your weekend?

Weekend past me khatam ho gaya, isliye was.

✗ I went to home.

✓ I went home.

Home ke pehle to nahi lagta.

✗ Do you like work from home?

Practice 1: pattern fill-ins

Blank me ek word likho. Capital letters aur extra spaces ki chinta mat karo. Check dabao, feedback dekho, phir agla.

Practice 2: grammar fill-ins

Ek word ya short form likho. Contractions aur full forms dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Practice 4: Hinglish → English

Hinglish prompt ka English version likho. Ek se zyada sahi answers accepted hain; wrong attempt par model answer dikhega.

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

I usually start work at ten.

My weekend was good, thanks. What about you?

I went to my cousin's place and stayed there for two hours.

I don't watch cricket very often, but I like it.

Model dialogue

Monday morning, office kitchen. Riya = colleague. Short answers do, phir question wapas poochho. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Morning! How was your weekend?

You

It was good, thanks. I went to my cousin's place. What about you?

Riya

Nice. I stayed home and watched a movie.

You

Do you like watching movies at home?

Riya

Sometimes. I usually go to the theatre.

You

Same here. I don't go very often, but I like it.

Har answer ke baad ek question poochho. Small talk tennis jaisa hai — ball wapas hit karo.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Your morning routine

Subah se office tak 4 lines bolo.

I usually wake up at ___ . I ___ at ___ . I usually start work at ___ .

2. Your weekend

Last weekend kya kiya, past simple me bolo.

My weekend was ___ . I went to ___ . I stayed ___ .

3. Ask them back

Teen small-talk questions bolo.

How was your weekend? Do you like ___ ? What about you?

4. Two-minute chat

Friend ke saath 2 minute conversation record karo.

Hi! How are you? ... What about you? ... Do you like ___ ?

60-second self-check

Phone me 60 seconds record karo. Neeche checklist se score karo; 5/6 = ready.

3/6 = ek pattern dobara bolo, phir record karo. Recording suno, ek cheez sudharo, dobara record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

-s endings

works = work + /s/; watches = watch + /ɪz/; goes = go + /z/. Practice: works, lives, watches.

Sentence rhythm

Content words par stress: I USUALLY START WORK at TEN. usually/at ko halka bolo.

was / were

was = /wɒz/ short; were = /wɜːr/. “It was good” me was ko lamba mat karo.

went

went = /went/, final /t/ clear. “I went home” ko “I wen home” mat bolo.
