The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. The cost is ___.

Total amount clearly bolo.

+
The cost is fifteen thousand rupees.
−
The cost isn't final yet.
?
What is the total cost?
  • The cost is twenty thousand rupees plus GST.Kharche bees hazaar rupaye plus GST hain.
  • The monthly cost is five thousand rupees.Monthly kharche paanch hazaar rupaye hain.

Grammar: cost is + amount. cost = total; price = ek item ka daam. hundred/thousand/lakh singular rehte hain.

Common mistake: “The cost is fifteen thousands” → “The cost is fifteen thousand.” Thousand singular.

2. Could you confirm ___?

Amount, date ya scope confirm karne ke liye.

+
Could you confirm the advance amount?
−
I can't confirm the discount yet.
?
Could you confirm the payment date?
  • Could you confirm the final amount?Kya aap final amount confirm kar sakte hain?
  • Could you confirm the invoice details?Kya aap invoice details confirm kar sakte hain?

Grammar: Could you confirm + noun/clause. Polite confirmation. confirm + thing, not person.

Common mistake: “Could you confirm me the amount?” → “Could you confirm the amount?” confirm + thing.

3. We need the payment.

Direct but polite — please add karo.

+
We need the payment before delivery.
−
We don't need the full amount now.
?
When can we expect the payment?
  • We need the advance to start the work.Kaam shuru karne ke liye advance chahiye.
  • We need the balance before the final handover.Final handover se pehle balance chahiye.

Grammar: need + noun. before + noun/-ing. Please se tone soft karo.

Common mistake: “We need to payment” → “We need the payment.” Noun ke pehle to nahi.

4. I'll send the invoice.

Invoice bhejne ka promise.

+
I'll send the invoice today.
−
I haven't sent the invoice yet.
?
Did you send the invoice?
  • I'll send the invoice on email.Main email par invoice bhej doonga.
  • I've sent the invoice on WhatsApp.Maine WhatsApp par invoice bhej diya hai.

Grammar: invoice = bill. send the invoice. by email / on WhatsApp.

Common mistake: “I'll send you invoice” → “I'll send you the invoice.” Article the chahiye.

5. When can we expect ___?

Payment ka polite follow-up.

+
When can we expect the payment?
−
I'm not sure when we can expect it.
?
When can we expect the next update?
  • When can we expect the advance?Advance kab tak expect kar sakte hain?
  • When can we expect the purchase order?Purchase order kab tak milega?

Grammar: When can + subject + base verb. expect + noun. Polite follow-up.

Common mistake: “When we can expect payment?” → “When can we expect the payment?” Can subject se pehle.

6. Is there any update on ___?

Soft follow-up — pressure kam, reminder clear.

+
Is there any update on the payment?
−
There is no update yet.
?
Is there any update on the invoice?
  • Is there any update on the advance?Advance par koi update hai?
  • Is there any update on the approval?Approval par koi update hai?

Grammar: Is there any update on + noun? Follow-up ka polite tarika.

Common mistake: “Any update on payment?” is okay informally, but “Is there any update on the payment?” polite hota hai.

Grammar corner

Ye rules Week 10 ke saare patterns cover karte hain. Ratta nahi — bolte time apply karo.

RuleExample
cost is + amount; thousand/lakh singularThe cost is fifteen thousand rupees.
Could you confirm + noun/clauseCould you confirm the amount?
need + noun (not to + noun)We need the payment.
will send the invoice; use the articleI'll send the invoice today.
When can + subject + base verb?When can we expect the payment?
Is there any update on + noun?Is there any update on the invoice?

Common mistakes

Ye 6 galtiyan Week 10 me sabse zyada hoti hain. Pehle wrong bolo, phir right — loud.

✗ The cost is fifteen thousands.

✓ The cost is fifteen thousand.

Thousand singular.

✗ Could you confirm me the amount?

✓ Could you confirm the amount?

confirm + thing.

✗ We need to payment.

✓ We need the payment.

need + noun.

✗ I'll send you invoice.

✓ I'll send you the invoice.

Article the.

✗ When we can expect payment?

✓ When can we expect the payment?

Can subject se pehle.

✗ Any update on payment?

Full polite question.

Model dialogue

Payment follow-up call. Riya = client. Invoice, total, advance aur discount — politely cover karo. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Hi, we haven't received the invoice yet.

You

Sorry about that. I'll send the invoice today.

Riya

What is the total cost?

You

The cost is fifteen thousand rupees, plus GST. We need the advance before we start.

Riya

Can you give a discount?

You

We can offer a five percent discount on the total. Could you confirm the advance by Friday?

Riya

I'll confirm tomorrow.

You

Great. Is there any update on the purchase order?

Money conversation me teen cheezein clear: amount, date, next step. Guess mat karo, confirm karo.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Share the cost

Total amount clearly bolo.

2. Ask for the advance

Advance politely maango.

3. Send the invoice

Invoice bhejne ka message bolo.

4. Payment follow-up

Do-line follow-up record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

Numbers

fifteen /fɪfˈtiːn/ vs fifty /ˈfɪfti/. Stress end me fifteen, start me fifty.

Lakh/crore vs million

1 lakh = 100,000; 1 crore = 10 million. Client Indian ho to lakh/crore, international ho to million bolo.

invoice

/ˈɪnvɔɪs/ — IN-voice. Stress first syllable par.

Linking

Could you → /wʊdʒə/. “Could you confirm” ko “could-ja confirm” jaisa jodo.
