The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. Can you repeat that, please?

Please add karo; tone polite rehta hai.

+
Can you repeat that, please?
−
Sorry, I can't hear you clearly.
?
Can you repeat that, please?
  • Can you repeat the last part, please?Kya aap last part dobara bata sakte hain?
  • Could you speak a little more slowly?Kya aap thoda dheere bol sakte hain?

Grammar: Can/Could + subject + base verb. Could zyada polite. Please sentence ke end me — tone soft rehta hai.

Common mistake: “Can you repeat?” → “Can you repeat that, please?” That se clear hota hai kya repeat karna hai.

2. What do you mean by ___?

Word ya phrase ka meaning puchhne ke liye.

+
What do you mean by scope?
−
I don't know what you mean.
?
What do you mean by scope?
  • What do you mean by 'by Friday'?‘Friday tak’ se aapka matlab kya hai?
  • Do you mean this week or next week?Aapka matlab is hafte ya agle hafte?

Grammar: mean by + noun/phrase. Confirm karne ke liye: Do you mean this week or next week?

Common mistake: “What you mean?” → “What do you mean?” Question me do chahiye.

3. Could you explain ___?

Explain ke baad kya explain karna hai, woh bolo.

+
Could you explain the payment process?
−
I don't understand the payment process.
?
Could you explain the payment process?
  • Could you explain the timeline?Kya aap timeline samjha sakte hain?
  • Could you explain it in simple words?Kya aap ise simple shabdon me samjha sakte hain?

Grammar: explain + thing. Person ke liye to: Could you explain it to me? ‘Explain me’ galat hai.

Common mistake: “Could you explain me?” → “Could you explain it to me?” Explain + thing + to + person.

4. Is it okay if I ___?

Permission maangne ka polite pattern.

+
Is it okay if I call you after lunch?
−
Is it okay if I don't join the call?
?
Is it okay if I call you after lunch?
  • Is it okay if I send it tomorrow?Kya main ise kal bhej sakta hoon?
  • Is it okay if we meet at four?Kya hum chaar baje mil sakte hain?

Grammar: Is it okay if + subject + present simple. Future ke liye will nahi lagta.

Common mistake: “Is it okay if I will call?” → “Is it okay if I call?” If clause me present simple.

5. Sorry, I didn't catch that.

Samajh nahi aaya ya sunai nahi diya — dono ke liye.

+
Sorry, I didn't catch that.
−
I didn't catch the last word.
?
Sorry, could you say that again?
  • Sorry, I didn't catch your name.Sorry, aapka naam nahi suna.
  • I didn't catch the last sentence.Aakhri sentence samajh nahi aaya.

Grammar: didn't + base verb. catch = sunna/samajhna. Past negative me didn't catch.

Common mistake: “I didn't caught that.” → “I didn't catch that.” didn't ke baad base verb.

6. How do you say ___ in English?

Hindi word ka English puchhne ke liye.

+
How do you say jaldi in English?
−
I don't know the English word for this.
?
How do you say this in English?
  • How do you say this word in English?Is word ko English me kya kehte hain?
  • How do you spell the domain name?Domain name ki spelling kaise likhte hain?

Grammar: How do you say + word + in English? Spelling ke liye: How do you spell it?

Common mistake: “How to say this in English?” → “How do you say this in English?” Question me do you.

Practice 1: pattern fill-ins

Blank me ek word likho. Capital letters aur extra spaces ki chinta mat karo. Check dabao, feedback dekho, phir agla.

Practice 2: grammar fill-ins

Ek word ya short form likho. Contractions aur full forms dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Practice 4: Hinglish → English

Hinglish prompt ka English version likho. Ek se zyada sahi answers accepted hain; wrong attempt par model answer dikhega.

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

Can you repeat that, please?

What do you mean by scope?

Could you explain the timeline?

Sorry, I didn't catch the last word.

Model dialogue

Client call, audio thoda unclear. Riya = client. Har line me ek clarifying question hai. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

So we need the new page by Friday.

You

Sorry, I didn't catch that. Can you repeat the date, please?

Riya

Friday, the 30th.

You

Thank you. And what do you mean by new page — the design or the content?

Riya

The design first.

You

Got it. Could you share the reference site?

Clarify karna stupid nahi lagta. Galat kaam karna lagta hai.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Ask for repetition

Client fast bol raha hai. Do lines bolo.

Sorry, I didn't catch that. Can you repeat that, please?

2. Check meaning

Ek word ka meaning confirm karo.

What do you mean by ___ ? Do you mean ___ or ___ ?

3. Ask permission

Do polite requests bolo.

Is it okay if I ___ ? Could you explain ___ ?

4. Phone-call rescue

Char lines ka rescue script record karo.

Sorry, the audio is not clear. Can you repeat that? ... Thank you. Could you explain ___ ?

Vocabulary in action

Sirf word yaad nahi — collocation ke saath bolo. Apne 60-second answer me do words use karo.

repeat

Could you repeat the question?

mean

What do you mean by scope?

explain

explain the process

Could you explain the process?

clarify

60-second self-check

Phone me 60 seconds record karo. Neeche checklist se score karo; 5/6 = ready.

3/6 = ek pattern dobara bolo, phir record karo. Recording suno, ek cheez sudharo, dobara record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

Yes/No question intonation

Can you REpeat that? — end me voice upar. Request ko soft banata hai.

Wh- question intonation

WHAT do you MEAN by SCOPE? — end me voice neeche. Stress meaning par.

didn't catch

didn't = /ˈdɪdənt/, catch = /kætʃ/. Linking: “didn't catch” ek saath bolo, dono ko alag alag mat toda karo.

explain

Stress second syllable: ex-PLAIN. “EXplain” nahi.

Week 3 mission

Ek asli client ya colleague se English me ek clarifying question poochho — repeat, meaning, ya permission.

Mission ko Day 7 se pehle karo. Ek asli insaan ke saath, ya phone par record karke. Perfect English nahi — clear English target hai.

Teacher ko follow-up poochho

Lesson ke baad ye questions ready rakho:

  • Client call me repeat maangna rude lagta hai kya?
  • “What do you mean by scope?” ka aur natural version kya hai?
  • Meri “Could you explain” line me tone polite thi?
  • Phone par audio clear karne ke liye aur kaunse phrases use karun?
  • “I didn't catch that” ke baad kya bolna chahiye?
