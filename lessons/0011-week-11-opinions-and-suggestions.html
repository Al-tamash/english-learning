The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. I think ___.

Opinion ka sabse simple starter.

+
I think the blue design looks better.
−
I don't think we need a new logo.
?
What do you think?
  • I think we should start with the homepage.Mujhe lagta hai humein homepage se shuru karna chahiye.
  • I don't think the price is too high.Mujhe nahi lagta price zyada hai.

Grammar: I think + clause. Negative often I don't think + clause, not I think not.

Common mistake: “I think that is better design” → “I think the blue design is better.” Clear subject rakho.

2. In my opinion, ___.

Thoda formal opinion — meetings ke liye.

+
In my opinion, we should start with the homepage.
−
In my opinion, this isn't the right time.
?
What's your opinion?
  • In my opinion, the simpler design is better.Mere hisaab se simple design better hai.
  • In my opinion, we need more testing.Mere hisaab se humein zyada testing chahiye.

Grammar: In my opinion, + clause. Comma ke baad complete sentence.

Common mistake: “In my opinion is better” → “In my opinion, it is better.” Opinion ke baad clause chahiye.

3. Why don't we ___?

Friendly suggestion, demand nahi.

+
Why don't we start with the homepage?
−
Why don't we wait for the feedback?
?
Why don't we meet on Monday?
  • Why don't we ask the client?Client se kyun nahi poochhte?
  • Why don't we test it first?Pehle test kyun nahi karte?

Grammar: Why don't we + base verb = suggestion. Real question nahi, idea hai.

Common mistake: “Why we don't start?” → “Why don't we start?” Question word + don't + subject.

4. I agree / I'm not sure.

Agreement aur polite disagreement dono.

+
I agree with you.
−
I'm not sure about that.
?
Do you agree?
  • I agree with your point.Main aapki baat se sehmat hoon.
  • I'm not sure about the price.Main price ke baare me sure nahi hoon.

Grammar: agree with + person; agree to + plan; agree on + decision. I'm not sure = polite disagreement.

Common mistake: “I am agree” → “I agree.” Agree khud verb hai, isliye am nahi.

5. What if we ___?

Alternative option offer karo.

+
What if we use a simpler design?
−
What if we don't change it?
?
What if we ask the client?
  • What if we test both options?Kya hum dono options test karein?
  • What if we move the deadline?Kya hum deadline aage badhayein?

Grammar: What if + subject + present simple = suggestion/possibility.

Common mistake: “What if we will use” → “What if we use.” If clause me present simple.

6. That sounds good.

Idea accept karna, short aur natural.

+
That sounds good.
−
That doesn't sound right.
?
Does that sound good?
  • That sounds good. Let's do it.Ye achha lagta hai. Chalo karte hain.
  • That sounds expensive.Ye mehnga lagta hai.

Grammar: sound + adjective. That sounds good/great/expensive/right.

Common mistake: “That sounds well” → “That sounds good.” Sound ke baad adjective.

Grammar corner

Ye rules Week 11 ke saare patterns cover karte hain. Ratta nahi — bolte time apply karo.

RuleExample
I think + clauseI think the blue design is better.
In my opinion, + clauseIn my opinion, we should start small.
Why don't we + base verb?Why don't we ask the client?
agree with person / agree to planI agree with you. I agree to the plan.
What if + present simple?What if we test it?
sound + adjectiveThat sounds good.

Common mistakes

Ye 6 galtiyan Week 11 me sabse zyada hoti hain. Pehle wrong bolo, phir right — loud.

✗ I am agree.

✓ I agree.

agree khud verb hai.

✗ In my opinion is better.

✓ In my opinion, it is better.

Opinion ke baad clause.

✗ Why we don't start?

✓ Why don't we start?

don't before subject.

✗ What if we will use a simpler design?

✓ What if we use a simpler design?

If clause me present simple.

✗ That sounds well.

✓ That sounds good.

sound + adjective.

✗ I think that is better design.

✓ I think the blue design is better.

Clear subject + adjective.

Practice 1: pattern fill-ins

Blank me ek word likho. Capital letters aur extra spaces ki chinta mat karo. Check dabao, feedback dekho, phir agla.

Practice 2: grammar fill-ins

Ek word ya short form likho. Contractions aur full forms dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Practice 4: Hinglish → English

Hinglish prompt ka English version likho. Ek se zyada sahi answers accepted hain; wrong attempt par model answer dikhega.

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

I think the blue design looks better.

In my opinion, we should start small.

Why don't we ask the client?

That sounds good.

Model dialogue

Design meeting. Riya = client. Opinion, polite disagreement aur better option — teeno use karo. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

We have two designs. Which one do you prefer?

You

I think the blue design looks better. It feels more professional.

Riya

I'm not sure. The orange one is more visible.

You

That's a good point. What if we use blue for the header and orange for the button?

Riya

That sounds good. Should we test both with users?

You

I agree. In my opinion, a small test is better than a long debate.

Opinion ka formula: I think + reason. Disagreement ka formula: That's a good point + What if we...?

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Give an opinion

Design par apni opinion do.

I think ___ . In my opinion, ___ .

2. Disagree politely

Sehmat na ho, but softly bolo.

That's a good point, but I'm not sure about ___ . What if we ___ ?

3. Suggest an option

Ek better option do.

Why don't we ___ ? What if we ___ ?

4. Close with agreement

Meeting me decision close karo.

That sounds good. I agree with ___ . Let's ___ .

Vocabulary in action

Sirf word yaad nahi — collocation ke saath bolo. Apne 60-second answer me do words use karo.

opinion

in my opinion

In my opinion, the design is clean.

idea

a good idea

That's a good idea.

better

a better option

The blue design is a better option.

option

another option

Let's try another option.

suggest

suggest a change

I suggest a smaller button.

agree

agree with you

I agree with you.

60-second self-check

Phone me 60 seconds record karo. Neeche checklist se score karo; 5/6 = ready.

3/6 = ek pattern dobara bolo, phir record karo. Recording suno, ek cheez sudharo, dobara record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

Contrastive stress

I think the BLUE design is better, not the ORANGE one. Contrast wale word par stress.

Softening

I'm not sure — voice soft, slow. “I disagree” se zyada polite lagta hai.

Linking

What if → /wɒtɪf/. Why don't we → /waɪdəʊntwi/. Suggestions me words jodo.

I think

Fast speech me “I think” halka hota hai: /aɪ θɪŋk/. Har word par stress mat do.
