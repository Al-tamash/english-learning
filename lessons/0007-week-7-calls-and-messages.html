The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. Can you hear me?

Call ki pehli line — audio check.

+
Can you hear me?
−
I can't hear you clearly.
?
Can you hear me now?
  • Can you hear me now?Ab aap mujhe sun sakte hain?
  • Sorry, I can't hear you. The audio is breaking.Sorry, main aapko sun nahi pa raha. Audio cut ho raha hai.

Grammar: hear = sunna (kaan se aana); listen = dhyan se sunna. Call check ke liye Can you hear me?

Common mistake: “Are you hearing me?” → “Can you hear me?” Hear ke saath can use karo.

2. I'm on a call.

Abhi call par busy ho.

+
I'm on a call right now.
−
I'm not on a call.
?
Are you on a call?
  • I'm on a call. Can I call you back?Main call par hoon. Kya main baad me call kar sakta hoon?
  • She's in a meeting right now.Woh abhi meeting me hai.

Grammar: on a call = call par busy; in a meeting = meeting me. State hai, isliye present continuous.

Common mistake: “I'm in call” → “I'm on a call.” on a call fixed phrase.

3. Let me share my screen.

Screen dikhane ka offer.

+
Let me share my screen.
−
I can't share my screen right now.
?
Can you share your screen?
  • Let me share my screen and show you the design.Main screen share karke aapko design dikhata hoon.
  • Can you see my screen?Kya aap meri screen dekh sakte hain?

Grammar: Let me + base verb = main karta hoon (offer). share my screen.

Common mistake: “Let me to share my screen” → “Let me share my screen.” let me ke baad base verb.

4. I'll call you back.

Baad me call karne ka promise.

+
I'll call you back in ten minutes.
−
I won't be able to call you back today.
?
Can I call you back?
  • I'll call you back after lunch.Main lunch ke baad call back karunga.
  • Can I call you back in five minutes?Kya main paanch minute me call back kar sakta hoon?

Grammar: call back = dobara call karna. will for promise. in + duration (in ten minutes).

Common mistake: “I'll call you back after ten minutes” → “I'll call you back in ten minutes.” in = duration ke baad.

5. Please check your email/WhatsApp.

File ya message bhejne ke baad follow-up.

+
Please check your email. I've sent the file.
−
I haven't sent it yet.
?
Did you get my email?
  • Please check your WhatsApp. I've sent the photo.Please WhatsApp check karo. Maine photo bhej di hai.
  • I've sent the invoice. Please check your email.Maine invoice bhej diya hai. Please email check karo.

Grammar: Please + base verb. check your email/WhatsApp. I've sent = just sent.

Common mistake: “Please check your email box” → “Please check your email.” email box ki zaroorat nahi.

6. Sorry, the connection is bad.

Network problem politely batao.

+
Sorry, the connection is bad.
−
The connection is better now.
?
Is the connection okay?
  • Sorry, the connection is bad. Can you repeat that?Sorry, connection kharab hai. Kya aap dobara bol sakte hain?
  • The connection is better now. Can you hear me?Ab connection better hai. Sunai de raha hai?

Grammar: connection is bad/poor/unstable. Sorry + statement. Blame network, not person.

Common mistake: “My connection is badly” → “The connection is bad.” Bad adjective, badly adverb.

Listen and repeat

Model dialogue

Video call, network thoda weak. Riya = client. Audio check, screen share aur follow-up cover karo. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Hi, can you hear me?

You

Hi Riya. Yes, I can hear you, but the connection is a little bad.

Riya

Should I call you on WhatsApp?

You

Let me try my headphones first. Okay, can you hear me now?

Riya

Yes, much better. Let me share my screen.

You

Great, I can see it. I'll send you the notes after the call.

Call ke teen checkpoints: audio, screen, next step. Har call ke end me follow-up message bolo.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Start a call

Call ki shuruaat karo.

Hi, can you hear me? ... Great. Let's start with ___ .

2. Bad network

Network problem politely batao.

Sorry, the connection is bad. Can you repeat that? ... Let me call you back in ___ .

3. Share your screen

Screen share ka offer karo.

Let me share my screen. Can you see it?

4. Follow-up message

Call ke baad 3-line message bolo.

Hi ___ , thanks for the call. Please check your email. I'll call you back on ___ .

Pronunciation quick check

Phone clarity

Final consonants clear rakho: hearD, senD, calL. Phone par ending chhup jati hai.

Pausing

Ek saans me sab mat bolo. “Sorry — the connection is bad — can you repeat that?” Har dash par chhota pause.

can / can't

can = /kən/ halka; can't = /kɑːnt/ clear. Phone par can't ko zyada clear bolo.

Linking

call you back → /kɔːljuːbæk/. “meet you” → /miːtʃuː/. Natural speech me words jodte hain.
