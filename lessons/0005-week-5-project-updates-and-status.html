The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. I've finished ___.

Kaam complete ho gaya, abhi result important hai.

+
I've finished the homepage design.
−
I haven't finished the contact page yet.
?
Have you finished the design?
  • I've finished the testing for the login page.Maine login page ki testing complete kar li hai.
  • She has finished the content update.Usne content update complete kar liya hai.

Grammar: Present perfect: have/has + past participle. Just-completed work with a present result. yet negative aur question ke end me.

Common mistake: “I have finish” → “I've finished.” have ke baad past participle.

2. I'm working on ___.

Abhi kaam chal raha hai.

+
I'm working on the payment page.
−
I'm not working on that today.
?
What are you working on?
  • I'm working on the mobile layout.Main mobile layout par kaam kar raha hoon.
  • We're working on the final testing.Hum final testing par kaam kar rahe hain.

Grammar: Present continuous: am/is/are + verb-ing. Abhi chal raha kaam.

Common mistake: “I working on it” → “I'm working on it.” be verb chahiye.

3. It will take ___.

Kitna time lagega, simple duration me bolo.

+
It will take two days.
−
It won't take long.
?
How long will it take?
  • It will take three hours to fix.Ise theek karne me teen ghante lagenge.
  • The design will take one week.Design me ek hafta lagega.

Grammar: will take + time. Duration: two days / three hours. How long...? se poochho.

Common mistake: “It will take two days time” → “It will take two days.” days ke baad time nahi.

4. We are waiting for ___.

Kisi cheez par ruka hua kaam.

+
We are waiting for the content from the client.
−
We aren't waiting for approval now.
?
What are we waiting for?
  • We are waiting for the logo files.Hum logo files ka wait kar rahe hain.
  • I'm waiting for your feedback.Main aapke feedback ka wait kar raha hoon.

Grammar: wait for + person/thing. Present continuous because wait abhi chal raha hai.

Common mistake: “We are waiting the client” → “We are waiting for the client.” wait for.

5. I'll send it by ___.

Deadline dene ke liye by.

+
I'll send it by Friday evening.
−
I won't send it before Monday.
?
When will you send it?
  • I'll send the report by 5 p.m.Main report shaam 5 baje tak bhej doonga.
  • We'll finish it by the end of the week.Hum ise hafte ke end tak khatam kar denge.

Grammar: by = deadline ke pehle ya us waqt tak. until = tab tak continuous. Deadline ke liye by.

Common mistake: “I'll send it on Friday” (deadline) → “I'll send it by Friday.” By = latest time.

6. The current status is ___.

Ek line me project ki position.

+
The current status is in review.
−
The current status is not clear.
?
What is the current status?
  • The current status is in progress.Abhi kaam chal raha hai.
  • The current status is on hold.Kaam abhi ruka hua hai.

Grammar: status is + noun/adjective. Common: in progress, in review, on hold, complete.

Common mistake: “The status is reviewing” → “The status is in review.” Status khud review nahi karta.

Grammar corner

Ye rules Week 5 ke saare patterns cover karte hain. Ratta nahi — bolte time apply karo.

RuleExample
Present perfect: have/has + past participleI've finished the design.
Present continuous: am/is/are + -ingI'm working on the payment page.
will take + durationIt will take two days.
wait for + person/thingWe are waiting for the content.
by + deadlineI'll send it by Friday.
status is + in progress / in review / on hold / completeThe status is in review.

Common mistakes

Ye 6 galtiyan Week 5 me sabse zyada hoti hain. Pehle wrong bolo, phir right — loud.

✗ I have finish the design.

✓ I've finished the design.

have + past participle.

✗ I working on it.

✓ I'm working on it.

be verb + verb-ing.

✗ It will take two days time.

✓ It will take two days.

days ke baad time nahi.

✗ We are waiting the client.

✓ We are waiting for the client.

wait for + person.

✗ I'll send it on Friday. (deadline)

✓ I'll send it by Friday.

Deadline ke liye by.

✗ The status is reviewing.

✓ The status is in review.

Status ke liye in + noun.

Practice 1: pattern fill-ins

Blank me ek word likho. Capital letters aur extra spaces ki chinta mat karo. Check dabao, feedback dekho, phir agla.

Practice 2: grammar fill-ins

Ek word ya short form likho. Contractions aur full forms dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Practice 4: Hinglish → English

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

I've finished the homepage design.

I'm working on the mobile version now.

It will take two days.

I'll send it by Friday evening.

Model dialogue

Daily standup. Riya = project manager. Finished, in progress, blocked, deadline — chaar cheezein cover karo. Target lines ko apni details se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Quick update — where are we on the homepage?

You

I've finished the design. I'm working on the mobile version now.

Riya

How long will it take?

You

It will take two days. We are waiting for the content from your team.

Riya

I'll send it today. Can you share a status update by Friday?

You

Yes, I'll send it by Friday evening. The current status is in progress.

Update ka formula: finished + in progress + blocked + deadline. Char lines, no story.

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. What is finished

Do completed cheezein bolo.

I've finished ___ . I've also finished ___ .

2. What is in progress

Abhi ka kaam bolo.

I'm working on ___ . It will take ___ .

3. What is blocked

Kis cheez ka wait hai.

We are waiting for ___ from ___ .

4. 60-second status update

Poora update record karo.

Here is my update. I've finished ___ . I'm working on ___ . It will take ___ . I'll send it by ___ .

Vocabulary in action

Sirf word yaad nahi — collocation ke saath bolo. Apne 60-second answer me do words use karo.

progress

make progress

We are making good progress.

update

a status update

I'll send a status update by Friday.

complete

complete the design

I've completed the design.

pending

pending approval

The invoice is pending approval.

review

in review

The homepage is in review.

approve

approve the design

Please approve the design today.

60-second self-check

Phone me 60 seconds record karo. Neeche checklist se score karo; 5/6 = ready.

3/6 = ek pattern dobara bolo, phir record karo. Recording suno, ek cheez sudharo, dobara record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

-ed endings

finished = /ˈfɪnɪʃt/ (t sound); completed = /kəmˈpliːtɪd/ (id sound); moved = /muːvd/ (d sound). Teen alag endings suno.

Numbers and dates

two days, three hours, Friday the 30th. Numbers par stress, aur ‘th’ ko teeth ke beech se bolo.

Contractions

I've = /aɪv/, I'm = /aɪm/, I'll = /aɪl/. Fast speech me yahi natural hai.

Sentence stress

I've FINISHED the DESIGN. I'm WORKING on the PAYMENT page. Content words par stress.
