The 6 patterns

Pehle poora pattern bolo. Phir apni details me badal ke bolo. Suno button se model pronunciation sun sakte ho.

1. I work at ___ as a ___.

Company ke pehle “at”, role ke pehle “as”.

+
I work at Edone Solutions as a web developer.
−
I don't work at a big company.
?
Where do you work?
  • I work at a startup as a designer.Main ek startup me designer hoon.
  • She works at a bank as a manager.Woh ek bank me manager hai.

Grammar: Present simple permanent job ke liye. Company ke pehle at, role ke pehle as, aur singular job ke pehle a/an. He/she/it ke saath work → works.

Common mistake: “I work in Edone Solutions” → “I work at Edone Solutions.” Company ke pehle at.

2. I'm from ___ / I live in ___.

“From” ke baad original city, “live in” ke baad current city.

+
I'm from Nagpur, but I live in Pune.
−
I'm not from Delhi.
?
Where are you from?
  • My hometown is small and quiet.Mera hometown chhota aur shaant hai.
  • I'm based in Pune and work remotely.Main Pune me based hoon aur remotely kaam karta hoon.

Grammar: be from = origin; live in = current city. City ke pehle in. Where are you from? (be) / Where do you live? (do).

Common mistake: “I am from Nagpur only” → “I'm from Nagpur.” Yahan only ki zaroorat nahi.

3. I have ___ years of experience.

Years ke pehle number, experience ke pehle “of”.

+
I have three years of experience in web development.
−
I don't have much experience with backend.
?
How many years of experience do you have?
  • I have experience with responsive design.Mere paas responsive design ka experience hai.
  • I manage a small development team.Main ek chhoti development team manage karta hoon.

Grammar: have experience ke liye; he/she/it ke saath has. Experience uncountable: much experience, many years. Question me do, are nahi.

Common mistake: “I am having 3 years experience” → “I have 3 years of experience.” Experience state hai, action nahi.

4. I build ___ for ___.

“Build” ke baad kya banate ho, “for” ke baad kiske liye.

+
I build responsive websites for small businesses.
−
I don't build mobile apps.
?
What do you build?
  • We build e-commerce apps for local shops.Hum local dukaano ke liye e-commerce apps banate hain.
  • Our team builds custom solutions for clients.Hamari team clients ke liye custom solutions banati hai.

Grammar: Present simple job duties ke liye. I/we/they build; he/she/it builds. for = client/beneficiary. Our team builds — team singular.

Common mistake: “Our team build websites” → “Our team builds websites.” Team singular hai.

5. I'm good at ___ / I'm learning ___.

“Good at” ke baad skill. “Learning” ke baad bhi skill ya verb+ing.

+
I'm good at frontend development.
−
I'm not good at backend yet.
?
What are you good at?
  • I'm learning backend development.Main backend development seekh raha hoon.
  • I'm learning SEO and testing.Main SEO aur testing seekh raha hoon.

Grammar: good at + noun/-ing. I'm learning = abhi seekh raha hoon. Negative me yet: I'm not good at backend yet.

Common mistake: “I'm good in frontend” → “I'm good at frontend.” Fixed phrase good at.

6. Do you work ___? / What do you do?

Sawaal “Do you” se shuru karo. “What do you do?” ka matlab kaam kya karte ho.

+
I work in-house as a frontend developer.
−
I don't work for an agency.
?
Do you work here?
  • What do you do at this company?Aap is company me kya karte ho?
  • I'm a freelancer, so I work with different clients.Main freelancer hoon, isliye alag clients ke saath kaam karta hoon.

Grammar: Present simple questions: do/does + subject + base verb. What do you do? = profession. work at company / for client / with people / in field.

Common mistake: “What you do?” → “What do you do?” Question me do chahiye.

Grammar corner

Ye 6 rules Week 1 ke saare patterns cover karte hain. Ratta nahi — bolte time apply karo.

RuleExample
I/you/we/they + base verb; he/she/it + verb-s I work · She works
Questions: do/does + subject + base verb Do you work here? · Does she work here?
Negatives: don't/doesn't + base verb I don't work · She doesn't work
be: am/is/are + noun/adjective; questions invert I'm a developer. · Are you a developer?
Prepositions: at company · as role · from origin · in city/field · for client · with people · of experience I work at Edone as a developer.
a/an + singular job · good at + noun/-ing · experience uncountable, years countable I'm a developer. I'm good at design. I have three years of experience.

Common mistakes

Ye 8 galtiyan Week 1 me sabse zyada hoti hain. Pehle wrong bolo, phir right — loud.

✗ I am having 3 years experience.

✓ I have 3 years of experience.

Experience state hai, action nahi; of chahiye.

✗ I am good in frontend.

✓ I'm good at frontend.

good at fixed phrase.

✗ I work in Edone Solutions.

✓ I work at Edone Solutions.

Company ke pehle at.

✗ I am from Nagpur only.

✓ I'm from Nagpur.

English me only ki zaroorat nahi.

✗ I am working here since 3 years.

✓ I have worked here for 3 years.

for + duration; present perfect later.

✗ She work at a bank.

✓ She works at a bank.

He/she/it + -s.

✗ What you do?

✓ What do you do?

Question me do.

✗ I am web developer.

✓ I am a web developer.

Singular job ke pehle a/an.

Practice 1: pattern fill-ins

Blank me ek word likho. Capital letters aur extra spaces ki chinta mat karo. Check dabao, feedback dekho, phir agla.

Practice 2: grammar fill-ins

Ek word ya short form likho. “Doesn't” aur “does not” dono accepted hain.

Practice 3: build the sentence

Jumbled words ko sahi order me full sentence banao. Capital letters aur final punctuation ki chinta mat karo.

Practice 4: Hinglish → English

Hinglish prompt ka English version likho. Ek se zyada sahi answers accepted hain; wrong attempt par model answer dikhega.

Listen and repeat

Suno → 2 second pause → bolo. Do rounds: pehle text dekho, phir screen se nazar hatao.

I work at Edone Solutions as a web developer.

I'm from Nagpur, but I live in Pune.

I have three years of experience.

I'm good at frontend design, and I'm learning backend.

Model dialogue

Client intro. Riya = client, You = learner. Target lines ko apne intro se replace karke bolo.

Riya

Hi, I'm Riya from Acme. What do you do?

You

Hi, Riya. I'm [your name]. I work at Edone Solutions as a web developer.

Riya

Where are you from?

You

I'm from Nagpur, but I live in Pune.

Riya

How many years of experience do you have?

You

I have three years of experience in web development.

Last line ke baad ek question poochho: “Do you work with a tech team?”

4 speak prompts

Phone me 30–45 seconds record karo. Perfect nahi, clear hona chahiye.

1. Introduce yourself

Naam, city, role aur company batao.

Hi, I'm ___ . I'm from ___ , but I live in ___ . I work at ___ as a ___ .

2. Explain your work

Edone Solutions kya karti hai aur tum kya banate ho.

3. Talk about experience

Kitne saal ka experience hai aur kis cheez me.

4. Ask them back

Conversation ko aage badhao, sirf apne baare me mat bolo.

60-second self-check

Phone me 60 seconds record karo. Neeche checklist se score karo; 5/6 = ready.

3/6 = ek pattern dobara bolo, phir record karo. Recording suno, ek cheez sudharo, dobara record karo.

Pronunciation quick check

v vs w

v me upper teeth lower lip ko touch karte hain — “very”, “vine”. w me lips round hoti hain — “wary”, “wine”.

th

Tongue tip teeth ke beech rakho. Soft air: “think”, “three”. Voice ke saath: “this”, “there”.

Experience

Stress second syllable par: ex-PE-ri-ence. “Experience” ko “ex-peer-ience” ki tarah bolo, har syllable equal mat karo.

Numbers: three vs thirty

three = 3 (short), thirty = 30 (thir-ty). Practice: three years / thirty years.

Sentence stress

Content words par stress: I WORK at EDONE as a WEB DEVELOPER. at/as/a ko jaldi bolo.
