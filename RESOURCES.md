# English Resources

## Knowledge

- **Book: _English Grammar in Use_ by Raymond Murphy (Intermediate, 2nd Indian edition)** — owned.
  Use as a reference for the day's pattern, never as a front-to-back course. Open it only when a pattern note needs one clear rule.

- **Book: _High School English Grammar & Composition_ by Wren & Martin** — owned.
  Reference for form and usage when Murphy is not detailed enough. Not a speaking course.

- **Book: _Word Power Made Easy_ by Norman Lewis** — owned.
  Optional vocabulary roots. Not core to speaking; dip in after Week 6 if curious about word families.

- [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/)
  Definitions, UK/US audio, collocations, and examples. Use for every new word's pronunciation and one natural example.

- [British Council LearnEnglish](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/)
  Graded listening, grammar, and vocabulary. Use for extra listening at the right level, especially when a week feels fast.

- [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/)
  Short audio with transcripts, including 6 Minute English and pronunciation. Use for shadowing extras.

- [YouGlish](https://youglish.com/)
  Hear a word or phrase in real videos. Use when a phrase feels textbook-like and you want to hear how people actually say it.

- [Ozdic](https://ozdic.com/)
  Collocations. Use when a sentence is grammatically correct but sounds unnatural.

- [Sounds of Speech, University of Iowa](https://soundsofspeech.uiowa.edu/)
  Mouth-position animations for tricky sounds (th, v, w). Use during pronunciation-focus days.

- **Book: _English Pronunciation in Use_ by Mark Hancock** — optional.
  Structured pronunciation reference. Buy only if pronunciation becomes the main blocker; the app covers the basics.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/EnglishLearning](https://www.reddit.com/r/EnglishLearning/)
  Moderated questions and corrections. Use for "does this sound natural?" posts and quick feedback from experienced learners and teachers.

- [r/languagelearning](https://www.reddit.com/r/languagelearning/)
  Method and habit discussions. Use when motivation or routine needs a reset, not for English facts.

- **Edone colleagues and client calls** — the highest-value practice.
  Use for weekly missions: one real question, update, or clarification in English.

- **Local Toastmasters club** — optional.
  Structured speaking practice with feedback. Check a nearby club after Week 4 if real-world pressure is helping.

## Gaps

- No free, high-quality structured course for Indian-English client communication; the app fills this gap.
- No reliable free pronunciation scoring for Indian accents; the browser keyword check is a soft proxy, not a score.
- No local English-speaking community identified yet; revisit after Week 4.
