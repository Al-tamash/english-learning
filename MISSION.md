# Mission: Speak English at work and in everyday life

## Why

I am a 26-year-old web developer at Edone Solutions and a final-semester BCA student. Client calls, WhatsApp messages, video calls, and in-person meetings happen in English, and I freeze when I have to speak. Being able to speak easily will make my work, client relationships, and everyday confidence better.

## Success looks like

- Handle a 10-minute client call in English: introduce myself, give a project update, ask questions, handle a delay, follow up on payment, and schedule the next step.
- Explain what I do at Edone Solutions and how a website is built in simple English.
- Have a 2–3 minute casual conversation with a colleague or friend without switching to Hindi.
- Understand the main point of a work email or a simple English page and ask a clarifying question when I don't.
- Speak for 2 minutes about my work or weekend with understandable pronunciation.
- Read a simple English book or page and follow the idea without translating every sentence.

## Constraints

- 30 minutes/day; 15-minute short session on busy days. Job plus BCA final semester.
- Android phone always; Windows laptop sometimes. Chrome. Cloudflare account.
- Explanations in simple Hinglish; practice in English.
- I own Murphy (Intermediate), Wren & Martin, and Word Power Made Easy, but finishing them is not a prerequisite.
- No paid tools. Voice recordings stay private; speech check only with consent.

## Out of scope

- IELTS/TOEFL or any exam preparation.
- Native accent or accent removal.
- Advanced literary English, debate, or complex business negotiation.
- Completing all three grammar books.
- Academic essay writing.
