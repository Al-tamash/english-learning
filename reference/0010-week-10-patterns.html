Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
cost is + amount; thousand/lakh singularThe cost is fifteen thousand rupees.
Could you confirm + noun/clauseCould you confirm the amount?
need + noun (not to + noun)We need the payment.
will send the invoice; use the articleI'll send the invoice today.
When can + subject + base verb?When can we expect the payment?
Is there any update on + noun?Is there any update on the invoice?

6 patterns

1. The cost is ___.

+ The cost is fifteen thousand rupees.

− The cost isn't final yet.

? What is the total cost?

2. Could you confirm ___?

+ Could you confirm the advance amount?

− I can't confirm the discount yet.

? Could you confirm the payment date?

3. We need the payment.

+ We need the payment before delivery.

− We don't need the full amount now.

? When can we expect the payment?

4. I'll send the invoice.

+ I'll send the invoice today.

− I haven't sent the invoice yet.

? Did you send the invoice?

5. When can we expect ___?

+ When can we expect the payment?

− I'm not sure when we can expect it.

? When can we expect the next update?

6. Is there any update on ___?

+ Is there any update on the payment?

− There is no update yet.

? Is there any update on the invoice?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
The cost is fifteen thousands. The cost is fifteen thousand. Thousand singular.
Could you confirm me the amount? Could you confirm the amount? confirm + thing.
We need to payment. We need the payment. need + noun.
I'll send you invoice. I'll send you the invoice. Article the.
When we can expect payment? When can we expect the payment? Can subject se pehle.
Any update on payment? Is there any update on the payment? Full polite question.

Phrase bank

  • The cost is fifteen thousand rupees. — Kharche pandrah hazaar rupaye hain.
  • Could you confirm the advance? — Kya aap advance confirm kar sakte hain?
  • We need the payment before delivery. — Delivery se pehle payment chahiye.
  • I'll send the invoice today. — Main aaj invoice bhej doonga.
  • Is there any update on the payment? — Payment par koi update hai?
  • When can we expect the payment? — Payment kab tak expect kar sakte hain?
  • The balance is due on delivery. — Balance delivery par dena hai.
  • The price includes GST. — Price me GST shamil hai.
  • We can offer a five percent discount. — Hum paanch percent discount de sakte hain.
  • Please make the payment by Friday. — Please Friday tak payment kar dijiye.

Words in action

  • cost — kul kharcha the total cost · The total cost is fifteen thousand rupees.
  • price — daam the final price · The final price includes GST.
  • quote — likhit daam send a quote · I'll send the quote by Monday.
  • invoice — bill send the invoice · I'll send the invoice today.
  • payment — bhugtan make the payment · Please make the payment by Friday.
  • advance — pehle diya paisa an advance payment · We need a fifty percent advance.
  • balance — baaki paisa the balance amount · The balance is due on delivery.
  • GST — tax plus GST · The price is five thousand plus GST.
  • discount — chhoot a five percent discount · We can offer a five percent discount.
  • confirm — pakka karna confirm the payment · Please confirm the payment date.

Pronunciation notes

  • Numbers: fifteen /fɪfˈtiːn/ vs fifty /ˈfɪfti/. Stress end me fifteen, start me fifty.
  • Lakh/crore vs million: 1 lakh = 100,000; 1 crore = 10 million. Client Indian ho to lakh/crore, international ho to million bolo.
  • invoice: /ˈɪnvɔɪs/ — IN-voice. Stress first syllable par.
  • Linking: Could you → /wʊdʒə/. “Could you confirm” ko “could-ja confirm” jaisa jodo.
