Bol · Week 2 reference

Daily routine and small talk

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
usually/always/often/sometimes/never + base verbI usually start at ten.
am/is/are + usually + adjective/nounI'm usually busy on Monday.
don't/doesn't + base verbI don't watch TV very often.
Past simple: was/were; went/stayedIt was good. I went home.
like + noun / verb-ingI like working from home.
Questions: Do you + base verb? / How was ___?Do you like tea? How was your weekend?

6 patterns

1. I usually ___ at ___.

+ I usually check my email at nine.

− I don't usually check email at night.

? What do you usually do in the morning?

2. I don't ___ very often.

+ I watch cricket almost every weekend.

− I don't watch cricket very often.

? Do you watch cricket very often?

3. How was your weekend?

+ It was good, thanks.

− It wasn't very relaxing.

? How was your weekend?

4. I went to ___ / I stayed ___.

+ I went to my cousin's place and stayed there for two hours.

− I didn't go out on Sunday.

? Where did you go on Sunday?

5. Do you like ___?

+ I like working from home.

− I don't like waking up early.

? Do you like working from home?

6. What about you?

+ I'm a developer. What about you?

− I'm not very busy today. What about you?

? What about you?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
I am usually go to office at 10. I usually go to office at 10. Frequency adverb ke baad base verb.
I don't watched cricket. I don't watch cricket. don't ke baad base verb.
How is your weekend? How was your weekend? Weekend past me khatam ho gaya, isliye was.
I went to home. I went home. Home ke pehle to nahi lagta.
Do you like work from home? Do you like working from home? like ke baad verb-ing.
What about your? What about you? You subject aur object dono me same rehta hai.

Phrase bank

  • How was your weekend? — Weekend kaisa tha?
  • It was good, thanks. — Achha tha, thanks.
  • What about you? — Tumhara kya?
  • I usually start at ten. — Main aam taur par das baje shuru karta hoon.
  • I don't go very often. — Main bahut zyada baar nahi jaata.
  • I went to my cousin's place. — Main cousin ke ghar gaya.
  • I stayed home. — Main ghar par ruka.
  • Do you like working from home? — Ghar se kaam karna pasand hai?
  • Same here. — Mere saath bhi wahi.
  • Not much, just work. — Kuch khaas nahi, bas kaam.

Words in action

  • routine — roz ka kaam ka order morning routine · I have a simple morning routine.
  • commute — ghar se office ka safar a long commute · My commute takes forty minutes.
  • weekend — Saturday–Sunday over the weekend · I slept a lot over the weekend.
  • family — parivaar spend time with family · I spend Sunday with my family.
  • food — khana home-cooked food · I prefer home-cooked food.
  • movie — film watch a movie · We watched a movie on Sunday.
  • cricket — cricket watch cricket · I don't watch cricket very often.
  • gym — vyayamshala go to the gym · I go to the gym three times a week.
  • traffic — bheed aur gaadiyon ka jam heavy traffic · There was heavy traffic this morning.
  • sleep — neend get enough sleep · I don't get enough sleep on weekdays.

Pronunciation notes

  • -s endings: works = work + /s/; watches = watch + /ɪz/; goes = go + /z/. Practice: works, lives, watches.
  • Sentence rhythm: Content words par stress: I USUALLY START WORK at TEN. usually/at ko halka bolo.
  • was / were: was = /wɒz/ short; were = /wɜːr/. “It was good” me was ko lamba mat karo.
  • went: went = /went/, final /t/ clear. “I went home” ko “I wen home” mat bolo.
