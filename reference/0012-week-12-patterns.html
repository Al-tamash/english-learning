Fluency and real calls

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
Let me + base verbLet me summarize the points.
The main point is + noun/clauseThe main point is the deadline.
Before + subject + verb, main clauseBefore we finish, let's confirm.
follow up with person / on topicI'll follow up with the client.
Could we + base verb; schedule for + timeCould we schedule it for Monday?
Thanks for + noun/-ingThanks for your time.

6 patterns

1. Let me summarize.

+ Let me summarize the main points.

− I don't have a full summary yet.

? Can you summarize the call?

2. The main point is ___.

+ The main point is the deadline.

− The main point isn't the price.

? What is the main point?

3. Before we finish, ___.

+ Before we finish, let's confirm the next call.

− Before we finish, let's not forget the payment question.

? Before we finish, can we confirm the date?

4. I'll follow up with ___.

+ I'll follow up with the client tomorrow.

− I won't follow up until Monday.

? Who will follow up?

5. Could we schedule ___?

+ Could we schedule the next call for Monday?

− We can't schedule it this week.

? Could we schedule a call?

6. Thanks for your time.

+ Thanks for your time.

− I don't want to take more of your time.

? Do you have five more minutes?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
Let me to summarize. Let me summarize. let me + base verb.
The main point is about the deadline. The main point is the deadline. Direct noun better.
Before finish, let's confirm. Before we finish, let's confirm. Before + subject + verb.
I'll follow up to the client. I'll follow up with the client. follow up with person.
Could we schedule to Monday? Could we schedule it for Monday? schedule for + time.
Thanks for your times. Thanks for your time. Time uncountable.

Phrase bank

  • Let me summarize. — Main summarize karta hoon.
  • The main point is the deadline. — Main point deadline hai.
  • Before we finish, let's confirm the next call. — Finish se pehle next call confirm karein.
  • I'll follow up with the client. — Main client se follow up karunga.
  • Could we schedule it for Monday? — Kya hum ise Monday ke liye rakh sakte hain?
  • Thanks for your time. — Aapke time ke liye thanks.
  • That works for me. — Mere liye theek hai.
  • The next step is the design. — Agla kadam design hai.
  • I'll send the notes today. — Main aaj notes bhej doonga.
  • Does that work for you? — Kya ye aapke liye theek hai?

Words in action

  • summary — chhota saar a quick summary · Let me give you a quick summary.
  • action item — kaam jo karna hai the action items · We have three action items.
  • follow-up — baad me yaad dilana a follow-up email · I'll send a follow-up email.
  • schedule — time tay karna schedule a call · Let's schedule a call for Monday.
  • decide — faisla karna decide the next step · Let's decide the next step.
  • next step — agle kadam the next step · The next step is the design.
  • owner — zimmedar insaan the task owner · Who is the owner of this task?
  • deadline — aakhri tareekh meet the deadline · We will meet the deadline.
  • recap — short summary a quick recap · Here is a quick recap.
  • close — khatam karna close the call · Let's close the call with next steps.

Pronunciation notes

  • Pausing: Let me summarize — the main point is — the deadline. Pause se listener ko process karne ka time milta hai.
  • Rhythm: Content words par stress: LET me SUMmarize the MAIN POINT. Function words halke.
  • Final intonation: Statements me voice neeche. “Thanks for your time.” — end me confident fall.
  • Confidence: Speed slow rakho. Bhool gaye to “Let me check that and follow up” bolo — pause se better hai.
