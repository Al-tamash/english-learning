Calls and messages

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
hear vs listen; Can you hear me?Can you hear me? I can't hear you.
on a call / in a meetingI'm on a call. She's in a meeting.
Let me + base verbLet me share my screen.
call back; in + durationI'll call you back in ten minutes.
Please + base verb; check your emailPlease check your email.
connection is bad/poorSorry, the connection is bad.

6 patterns

1. Can you hear me?

+ Can you hear me?

− I can't hear you clearly.

? Can you hear me now?

2. I'm on a call.

+ I'm on a call right now.

− I'm not on a call.

? Are you on a call?

3. Let me share my screen.

+ Let me share my screen.

− I can't share my screen right now.

? Can you share your screen?

4. I'll call you back.

+ I'll call you back in ten minutes.

− I won't be able to call you back today.

? Can I call you back?

5. Please check your email/WhatsApp.

+ Please check your email. I've sent the file.

− I haven't sent it yet.

? Did you get my email?

6. Sorry, the connection is bad.

+ Sorry, the connection is bad.

− The connection is better now.

? Is the connection okay?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
Are you hearing me? Can you hear me? Hear ke saath can.
I'm in call. I'm on a call. on a call fixed phrase.
Let me to share my screen. Let me share my screen. let me + base verb.
I'll call you back after ten minutes. I'll call you back in ten minutes. in + duration.
Please check your email box. Please check your email. email box ki zaroorat nahi.
My connection is badly. The connection is bad. bad adjective, badly adverb.

Phrase bank

  • Can you hear me? — Kya aap mujhe sun sakte hain?
  • Sorry, the connection is bad. — Sorry, connection kharab hai.
  • Let me share my screen. — Main screen share karta hoon.
  • Can you see my screen? — Kya aap meri screen dekh sakte hain?
  • I'll call you back in ten minutes. — Main das minute me call back karunga.
  • I'm on a call right now. — Main abhi call par hoon.
  • Please check your email. — Please email check karo.
  • Please check your WhatsApp. — Please WhatsApp check karo.
  • You're on mute. — Aapka mute on hai.
  • I'll send you the notes after the call. — Call ke baad notes bhej doonga.

Words in action

  • mute — awaz band on mute · You're on mute.
  • screen — display share my screen · Let me share my screen.
  • connection — network ka link a bad connection · Sorry, the connection is bad.
  • message — sandesh send a message · I'll send you a message.
  • email — electronic mail check your email · Please check your email.
  • WhatsApp — messaging app on WhatsApp · I'll send it on WhatsApp.
  • voice note — bol kar bheja message send a voice note · I'll send a voice note.
  • ring — phone bajna the phone is ringing · The phone is ringing. Can I call you back?
  • call back — dobara call karna call you back · I'll call you back in ten minutes.

Pronunciation notes

  • Phone clarity: Final consonants clear rakho: hearD, senD, calL. Phone par ending chhup jati hai.
  • Pausing: Ek saans me sab mat bolo. “Sorry — the connection is bad — can you repeat that?” Har dash par chhota pause.
  • can / can't: can = /kən/ halka; can't = /kɑːnt/ clear. Phone par can't ko zyada clear bolo.
  • Linking: call you back → /kɔːljuːbæk/. “meet you” → /miːtʃuː/. Natural speech me words jodte hain.
