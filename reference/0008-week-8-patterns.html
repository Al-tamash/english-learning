In-person meetings

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
Nice to meet you / Nice meeting youNice to meet you. Nice meeting you.
Let me + base verbLet me show you the office.
Would you like + noun / to + verbWould you like some tea?
can offer + person + thingWe can offer you a discount.
Here is/are + nounHere is the list. Here are the samples.

6 patterns

1. Nice to meet you.

+ Nice to meet you, Riya.

− It's not our first meeting — we spoke on Zoom.

? Is this your first visit to our office?

2. Let me show you ___.

+ Let me show you our office.

− I can't show you the server room.

? Can you show me the design?

3. Would you like ___?

+ Would you like some tea?

− No, thank you. I don't want anything right now.

? Would you like to see the demo?

4. We can offer ___.

+ We can offer a ten percent discount.

− We can't offer free hosting.

? What can you offer?

5. Here is ___.

+ Here is our price list.

− This isn't the final quote.

? Is this the final price?

6. What do you think?

+ What do you think about the design?

− I'm not sure what you think.

? What do you think?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
Nice to meet to you. Nice to meet you. meet ke baad to nahi.
Let me to show you the office. Let me show you the office. let me + base verb.
Do you like some water? Would you like some water? Offer ke liye would you like.
We can offer to you a discount. We can offer you a discount. offer + person + thing.
Here is our samples. Here are our samples. Plural ke liye here are.
What do you think about? What do you think about it? about ke baad object chahiye.

Phrase bank

  • Nice to meet you. — Aapse mil kar achha laga.
  • Thank you for coming. — Aane ke liye thanks.
  • Let me show you our office. — Main aapko hamara office dikhata hoon.
  • Would you like some tea? — Kya aap chai lena chahenge?
  • Here is our price list. — Ye hamari price list hai.
  • We can offer you a discount. — Hum aapko discount de sakte hain.
  • What do you think about the design? — Design ke baare me kya sochte hain?
  • Please feel comfortable. — Aaram se baithiye.
  • I'll send you the final quote. — Main final quote bhej doonga.
  • Thank you for your time. — Aapke time ke liye thanks.

Words in action

  • office — kaam ki jagah visit our office · Thank you for visiting our office.
  • cabin — alag kamra the manager's cabin · The manager's cabin is on the first floor.
  • sample — namuna a product sample · Here is a sample of the design.
  • demo — dikha kar samjhana give a demo · Let me give you a quick demo.
  • price — daam the final price · Here is our final price.
  • offer — peshkash a special offer · We can offer a special price.
  • discount — chhoot a ten percent discount · We can give a ten percent discount.
  • visit — aana / mulaqat a client visit · Thank you for your visit.
  • tour — ghoom kar dikhana an office tour · Let me give you a quick tour.
  • comfortable — aaram se feel comfortable · Please feel comfortable and ask questions.

Pronunciation notes

  • Polite intonation: Would you LIKE some TEA? — voice end me soft up. Demand nahi, offer lagta hai.
  • Prices: ten percent = /ten pəˈsent/. Fifteen vs fifty: fifTEEN (stress end), FIFty (stress start).
  • Linking: meet you → /miːtʃuː/; would you → /wʊdʒə/. In-person me bhi natural linking chahiye.
  • Numbers: 1,500 = fifteen hundred; 15,000 = fifteen thousand. Lakh/crore ko client ke saath clear karo.
