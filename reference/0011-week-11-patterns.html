6 patterns

1. I think ___.

+ I think the blue design looks better.

− I don't think we need a new logo.

? What do you think?

2. In my opinion, ___.

+ In my opinion, we should start with the homepage.

− In my opinion, this isn't the right time.

? What's your opinion?

3. Why don't we ___?

+ Why don't we start with the homepage?

− Why don't we wait for the feedback?

? Why don't we meet on Monday?

4. I agree / I'm not sure.

+ I agree with you.

− I'm not sure about that.

? Do you agree?

5. What if we ___?

+ What if we use a simpler design?

− What if we don't change it?

? What if we ask the client?

6. That sounds good.

+ That sounds good.

− That doesn't sound right.

? Does that sound good?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
I am agree. I agree. agree khud verb hai.
In my opinion is better. In my opinion, it is better. Opinion ke baad clause.
Why we don't start? Why don't we start? don't before subject.
What if we will use a simpler design? What if we use a simpler design? If clause me present simple.
That sounds well. That sounds good. sound + adjective.
I think that is better design. I think the blue design is better. Clear subject + adjective.

Phrase bank

  • I think the blue design is better. — Mujhe lagta hai blue design better hai.
  • In my opinion, we should start small. — Mere hisaab se chhote se shuru karna chahiye.
  • Why don't we ask the client? — Client se kyun nahi poochhte?
  • I agree with you. — Main aapse sehmat hoon.
  • I'm not sure about that. — Main us baare me sure nahi hoon.
  • What if we test both options? — Kya hum dono options test karein?
  • That sounds good. — Ye achha lagta hai.
  • That's a good point. — Ye achhi baat hai.
  • I prefer the simpler design. — Mujhe simple design zyada pasand hai.
  • Let's decide by Friday. — Chalo Friday tak decide karte hain.

Words in action

  • opinion — ray in my opinion · In my opinion, the design is clean.
  • idea — soch a good idea · That's a good idea.
  • better — behtar a better option · The blue design is a better option.
  • option — vikalp another option · Let's try another option.
  • suggest — sujhav dena suggest a change · I suggest a smaller button.
  • agree — sehmat hona agree with you · I agree with you.
  • disagree — asahmat hona politely disagree · I politely disagree with that point.
  • maybe — shayad maybe we should · Maybe we should test it first.
  • possible — mumkin is it possible · Is it possible to finish by Friday?
  • prefer — zyada pasand karna prefer this design · I prefer the simpler design.

Pronunciation notes

  • Contrastive stress: I think the BLUE design is better, not the ORANGE one. Contrast wale word par stress.
  • Softening: I'm not sure — voice soft, slow. “I disagree” se zyada polite lagta hai.
  • Linking: What if → /wɒtɪf/. Why don't we → /waɪdəʊntwi/. Suggestions me words jodo.
  • I think: Fast speech me “I think” halka hota hai: /aɪ θɪŋk/. Har word par stress mat do.
