Project updates and status

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
Present perfect: have/has + past participleI've finished the design.
Present continuous: am/is/are + -ingI'm working on the payment page.
will take + durationIt will take two days.
wait for + person/thingWe are waiting for the content.
by + deadlineI'll send it by Friday.
status is + in progress / in review / on hold / completeThe status is in review.

6 patterns

1. I've finished ___.

+ I've finished the homepage design.

− I haven't finished the contact page yet.

? Have you finished the design?

2. I'm working on ___.

+ I'm working on the payment page.

− I'm not working on that today.

? What are you working on?

3. It will take ___.

+ It will take two days.

− It won't take long.

? How long will it take?

4. We are waiting for ___.

+ We are waiting for the content from the client.

− We aren't waiting for approval now.

? What are we waiting for?

5. I'll send it by ___.

+ I'll send it by Friday evening.

− I won't send it before Monday.

? When will you send it?

6. The current status is ___.

+ The current status is in review.

− The current status is not clear.

? What is the current status?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
I have finish the design. I've finished the design. have + past participle.
I working on it. I'm working on it. be verb + verb-ing.
It will take two days time. It will take two days. days ke baad time nahi.
We are waiting the client. We are waiting for the client. wait for + person.
I'll send it on Friday. (deadline) I'll send it by Friday. Deadline ke liye by.
The status is reviewing. The status is in review. Status ke liye in + noun.

Phrase bank

  • Here is my update. — Ye mera update hai.
  • I've finished the design. — Maine design complete kar liya hai.
  • I'm working on the mobile version. — Main mobile version par kaam kar raha hoon.
  • It will take two days. — Isme do din lagenge.
  • We are waiting for the content. — Hum content ka wait kar rahe hain.
  • I'll send it by Friday evening. — Main Friday shaam tak bhej doonga.
  • The current status is in progress. — Abhi kaam chal raha hai.
  • The page is ready for review. — Page review ke liye taiyar hai.
  • There is a small delay. — Thodi deri hai.
  • We will meet the deadline. — Hum deadline meet karenge.

Words in action

  • progress — aage badhna make progress · We are making good progress.
  • update — nayi jankari a status update · I'll send a status update by Friday.
  • complete — khatam karna complete the design · I've completed the design.
  • pending — baaki / ruka hua pending approval · The invoice is pending approval.
  • review — jaanch in review · The homepage is in review.
  • approve — manzoori dena approve the design · Please approve the design today.
  • deadline — aakhri tareekh meet the deadline · We will meet the deadline.
  • delay — deri a small delay · There is a small delay in testing.
  • ready — taiyar ready for review · The page is ready for review.
  • testing — jaanch parakh final testing · We are doing final testing.

Pronunciation notes

  • -ed endings: finished = /ˈfɪnɪʃt/ (t sound); completed = /kəmˈpliːtɪd/ (id sound); moved = /muːvd/ (d sound). Teen alag endings suno.
  • Numbers and dates: two days, three hours, Friday the 30th. Numbers par stress, aur ‘th’ ko teeth ke beech se bolo.
  • Contractions: I've = /aɪv/, I'm = /aɪm/, I'll = /aɪl/. Fast speech me yahi natural hai.
  • Sentence stress: I've FINISHED the DESIGN. I'm WORKING on the PAYMENT page. Content words par stress.
