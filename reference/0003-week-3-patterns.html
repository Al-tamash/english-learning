Asking and clarifying

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

6 patterns

1. Can you repeat that, please?

+ Can you repeat that, please?

− Sorry, I can't hear you clearly.

? Can you repeat that, please?

2. What do you mean by ___?

+ What do you mean by scope?

− I don't know what you mean.

? What do you mean by scope?

3. Could you explain ___?

+ Could you explain the payment process?

− I don't understand the payment process.

? Could you explain the payment process?

4. Is it okay if I ___?

+ Is it okay if I call you after lunch?

− Is it okay if I don't join the call?

? Is it okay if I call you after lunch?

5. Sorry, I didn't catch that.

+ Sorry, I didn't catch that.

− I didn't catch the last word.

? Sorry, could you say that again?

6. How do you say ___ in English?

+ How do you say jaldi in English?

− I don't know the English word for this.

? How do you say this in English?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
What you mean? What do you mean? Question me do chahiye.
Could you explain me? Could you explain it to me? explain + thing + to + person.
I didn't caught that. I didn't catch that. didn't ke baad base verb.
Is it okay if I will call? Is it okay if I call? If clause me present simple.
How to say this in English? How do you say this in English? Question me do you.
Can you repeat? Can you repeat that, please? That + please se request clear aur polite hoti hai.

Phrase bank

  • Can you repeat that, please? — Kya aap dobara bata sakte hain?
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that. — Sorry, woh samajh nahi aaya.
  • What do you mean by scope? — Scope se aapka matlab kya hai?
  • Could you explain the timeline? — Kya aap timeline samjha sakte hain?
  • Is it okay if I call later? — Kya main baad me call kar sakta hoon?
  • Could you speak slowly, please? — Kya aap dheere bol sakte hain?
  • How do you say this in English? — Ise English me kya kehte hain?
  • How do you spell it? — Iski spelling kaise hai?
  • Do you mean this week or next week? — Aapka matlab is hafte ya agle hafte?
  • I want to confirm the deadline. — Main deadline confirm karna chahta hoon.

Words in action

  • repeat — dobara bolna repeat the question · Could you repeat the question?
  • mean — matlab hona mean by scope · What do you mean by scope?
  • explain — samjhana explain the process · Could you explain the process?
  • clarify — spasht karna clarify one point · I'd like to clarify one point.
  • example — udaharan give an example · Could you give an example?
  • detail — vistar in detail · Could you explain it in detail?
  • understand — samajhna understand the requirement · I understand the requirement now.
  • slowly — dheere speak slowly · Could you speak slowly, please?
  • spelling — spelling check the spelling · Can you check the spelling of the domain?
  • confirm — pakka karna confirm the deadline · I want to confirm the deadline.

Pronunciation notes

  • Yes/No question intonation: Can you REpeat that? — end me voice upar. Request ko soft banata hai.
  • Wh- question intonation: WHAT do you MEAN by SCOPE? — end me voice neeche. Stress meaning par.
  • didn't catch: didn't = /ˈdɪdənt/, catch = /kætʃ/. Linking: “didn't catch” ek saath bolo, dono ko alag alag mat toda karo.
  • explain: Stress second syllable: ex-PLAIN. “EXplain” nahi.

Week 3 mission
