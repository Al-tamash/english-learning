Problems, delays, apologies

6 patterns · 6 grammar rules · 6 mistakes · 10 phrases · 10 words. Client call se pehle 2 minute is sheet par eye scan karo, phir loud bolo.

Grammar quick rules

RuleExample
sorry for + noun/-ingI'm sorry for the delay.
There was/were + noun; issue withThere was an issue with the server.
because + clause; because of + nounIt's delayed because the content is late.
Present continuous for a current fixWe're fixing it right now.
update + person (no to)I'll update you by Friday.
Thank you for + noun/-ingThank you for your patience.

6 patterns

1. I'm sorry for the delay.

+ I'm sorry for the delay.

− I'm not happy about the delay either.

? Is there a delay?

2. There was an issue with ___.

+ There was an issue with the payment gateway.

− There wasn't any issue with the design.

? Was there an issue with the server?

3. It's delayed because ___.

+ It's delayed because the content is not ready.

− It's not delayed because of us.

? Why is it delayed?

4. We're fixing it.

+ We're fixing it right now.

− We're not fixing the design today.

? When will it be fixed?

5. I'll update you by ___.

+ I'll update you by 4 p.m. today.

− I won't have an update before lunch.

? When will you update me?

6. Thank you for your patience.

+ Thank you for your patience.

− Sorry for the confusion.

? Can you give us a little more time?

Common mistakes

WrongRightWhy
Sorry for delay. Sorry for the delay. Article the.
There was issue with server. There was an issue with the server. a/the article.
It's delayed because the content not ready. It's delayed because the content is not ready. Clause me verb.
We fixing it. We're fixing it. be + verb-ing.
I'll update to you by Friday. I'll update you by Friday. update + person, to nahi.
Thank you for your patient. Thank you for your patience. patience = sabr.

Phrase bank

  • I'm sorry for the delay. — Deri ke liye sorry.
  • There was an issue with the server. — Server me problem thi.
  • It's delayed because the content is not ready. — Delay hui kyunki content taiyar nahi hai.
  • We're fixing it right now. — Hum ise abhi theek kar rahe hain.
  • It will be fixed in one hour. — Ye ek ghante me theek ho jayega.
  • I'll update you by 4 p.m. — Main 4 baje tak update doonga.
  • Thank you for your patience. — Aapke sabr ke liye thanks.
  • I understand your concern. — Main aapki chinta samajhta hoon.
  • We will make sure it doesn't happen again. — Hum dhyan rakhenge ki dobara na ho.
  • Sorry for the confusion. — Confusion ke liye sorry.

Words in action

  • issue — problem a technical issue · There was a technical issue with the server.
  • bug — software ki galti fix a bug · We fixed the login bug.
  • delay — deri a short delay · There is a short delay in testing.
  • fix — theek karna fix the problem · We're fixing the problem now.
  • solve — hal karna solve the issue · We solved the issue in one hour.
  • mistake — galti a small mistake · There was a small mistake in the invoice.
  • patience — sabr thank you for your patience · Thank you for your patience.
  • update — nayi jankari send an update · I'll send an update by 4 p.m.
  • apologize — sorry bolna apologize for the delay · I apologize for the delay.
  • reason — wajah the main reason · The main reason is the late content.

Pronunciation notes

  • Tone in apologies: I'm SO sorry for the deLAY. Voice thodi neeche, slow. Fast apology rude lagti hai.
  • patience: /ˈpeɪʃns/ — PEI-shns. ‘patient’ (/ˈpeɪʃnt/) se ending different hai.
  • because: /bɪˈkɒz/ — stress second syllable. “be-CUZ” nahi, “be-CAWZ”.
  • Pausing: Sorry — there was an issue — we're fixing it. Pause se message clear aur calm lagta hai.
